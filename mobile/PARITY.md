## App-only

- Native tab bar with Liquid Glass and the live workout in the tab bar's accessory (iOS 26+).
- Offline sign-in with this phone's last account; durable workout draft and finished-workout queue on the device.
- Renaming custom workout types (the web can only add, recolour and remove them).

## Known platform gaps

- **App version reporting:** the app doesn't send `X-Relay-Version` yet, so **Admin › App versions in use** shows it as _Not reported_ and `MIN_WRITE_CLIENT_VERSION` can't tell it apart. It needs a version scheme first: the app is 0.1.0 while web releases are 1.x.
- **Presence:** the app doesn't open the web's presence connection, so friends don't see it online or see its workout in progress, and Social re-reads friends and the feed on a timer instead of live.
- **Background rest alerts and the Dynamic Island countdown:** need `expo-notifications` and, for the Live Activity, an EAS development build.
